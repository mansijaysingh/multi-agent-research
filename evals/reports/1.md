# impact of AI on customer support

## Introduction

Artificial intelligence is transforming customer interactions by making them faster, more accessible, and more efficient through the quick addressing of queries, reduced wait times, and the automation of repetitive tasks [1]. While AI adoption brings significant benefits, challenges such as ethics and data privacy must be taken into account [1]. Rather than replacing human staff, businesses utilize AI tools to augment human capabilities, combining technological speed with human empathy [3].

## Key Findings

AI implementation significantly improves operational efficiency and productivity. A Salesforce survey indicates that 63% of service professionals believe generative AI aids in serving customers faster, and Gartner predicts that 80% of customer service and support organizations will utilize generative AI by 2025 to enhance the customer experience and agent productivity [1]. Research from the National Bureau of Economic Research shows that AI access raises customer support professional productivity by an average of 14 percent [3]. Furthermore, AI-powered assistants can save over 430 agent hours, raise closed cases by up to 30 percent, and cut issue resolution time in half [2]. 

Support operations benefit financially and structurally from AI. Artificial intelligence lowers operational costs, decreases the cost per transaction, and increases scalability and overall business impact [2][4]. By automating repetitive tasks like ticket triaging and answering frequent inquiries, AI tools enable support teams to achieve more with fewer resources [2]. This technology also optimizes data collection and removes redundancies, allowing human staff to focus on complex or emotionally sensitive issues, proactive support, customer onboarding, and premium support [2][3][4]. Additionally, AI provides agents with real-time insights and suggested next steps during conversations [3].

From the customer perspective, AI enhances satisfaction, accessibility, and availability [2][4]. AI tools enable round-the-clock availability and provide instant replies to lower wait times [2][3]. Technologies such as multilingual chatbots and voice assistants also improve accessibility for customers facing language barriers or disabilities [3]. Businesses are shifting from traditional volume-based metrics like ticket counts to holistic indicators such as customer satisfaction and AI resolution rates [4]. Advanced implementations, such as agentic AI workflows treated similarly to human employees with performance reviews, have resulted in faster problem resolutions, happier customers, and positively impacted company renewal rates [5].

## Conclusion

The integration of artificial intelligence into customer support fundamentally alters how businesses interact with their customers and measure return on investment [1][4]. By handling routine tasks and augmenting human capabilities, AI increases productivity, reduces operational costs, and elevates customer satisfaction [2][3][4]. As organizations increasingly rely on these technologies to meet evolving needs, AI continues to prove essential for driving efficiency and scalability in modern customer service [1][4].
